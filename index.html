Server Rules

The Golden Rule

Don't be a Dick.

It's quite simple, really. Make good decisions.

Use common sense, respect other players, and remember that everyone is here to enjoy their roleplay experience. If you're unsure whether something is acceptable, consider how it would affect the experience of others.

Final Reminder

These rules exist to protect fair, immersive, and enjoyable roleplay for the entire community. Staff reserve the right to review situations individually and take appropriate action where behaviour undermines the server or the experience of other players.

Use common sense, respect the roleplay, and make Revival Roleplay a world worth being part of.
